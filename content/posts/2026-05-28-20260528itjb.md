**为什么重要：** 这一数据表明，尽管伊朗互联网部分恢复，但网络活动仍远低于正常水平。

> **EN — Iran's Internet is partially restored, Cloudflare Radar data shows**
>
> Cloudflare Radar data indicates that nearly three months after the shutdown began in Iran, network activity has recovered to 40% of pre-shutdown levels.
